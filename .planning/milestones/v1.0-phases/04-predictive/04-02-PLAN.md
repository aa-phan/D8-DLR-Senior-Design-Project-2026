<objective>
Turn the tracer's honest-but-narrow forecast into the real C9 deliverable: a band derived from a
walk-forward backtest rather than an in-sample fit, a second smoothing method behind the same
signature, and forecast output published through an additively-versioned `PIPELINE_SCHEMA` instead of
an ad hoc dict.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 4 Success Criteria 1 and 2, and requirement DS-F11. The tracer proved the wiring;
this plan makes the number defensible. The band is the part that matters most — a rating system that
overstates its own certainty is the failure mode DS-NF5 exists to prevent, and an in-sample residual
band is exactly the "too good to be true" signature RESEARCH Pitfall 1 warns about.

Output: `ampacity_forecast.py` with a real expanding-window backtest and both Tier 1 methods,
`PIPELINE_SCHEMA` at version 2 with five additive optional columns, `read_history` validating before
training, and three test files covering all of it against a deterministic multi-week synthetic series.
</objective>

<objective>
Turn a corridor from a name into a rated line: define the two D-01 corridors in config, discretize each
into 5 geodesic points, resolve each point to a nearby weather station, fetch that point's own weather,
compute its ampacity through the Plan-03-01 safety monitor, and reduce the 5 values to a corridor-level
rating with `min()`.

Purpose: DS-F10 and ROADMAP Phase 3 Success Criteria 3 and 4. No notion of a line, a route, or a
per-point ampacity reduction exists anywhere in the codebase today — `station_explorer.CORRIDORS` only
ranks stations against five fixed points. This plan builds C8 by composing three patterns that already
exist here (`haversine_km`'s math style, `rank_by_corridor`'s proximity ranking,
`process_dlr_to_hourly`'s `.min()` weakest-link reduction) rather than inventing new algorithms.

Output: `backend/corridor_discretization.py` (new C8 module) plus its test file, and the corridor
endpoint definitions that make `CORRIDOR_ENDPOINTS` the single geographic model for the whole system.
</objective>

through the `clean_history(history, as_of=end)` call 02-01 wired into `city_pipeline`; this plan
does not touch `city_pipeline.py`.

The gap strategy decided here is "mark, never fill": a missing slot is never imputed, the first
row after it carries `after_gap` and `gap_slots`, and the report measures the trailing gap and the
newest unbroken run so plan 02-04 can decide when a forecast built across a gap may gate G6.
Thresholds are provisional, written down with their reasoning, and not fitted to data (Phase 3
validates them).
Output: rules and constants in `backend/data_cleaning.py`, tests in
`backend/tests/data_cleaning_test.py`, the policy doc, and fixture-only changes to Phase 1's
`backend/tests/city_pipeline_test.py`.

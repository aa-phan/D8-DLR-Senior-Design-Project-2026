conductor-dependent candidates under any explicit conductor point and stores them as new, hashed
artifacts outside the frozen directory (SEL-02), and the ABLATION conductor-invariance driver that refits

registry in 07-10. Both modules are new; no frozen shadow file changes. Real runs happen in 07-11.

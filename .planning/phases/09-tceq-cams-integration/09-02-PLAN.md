<objective>
Expand outward from Plan 09-01's proven single-city, single-day slice to the full WX-01 surface:
multi-day window decomposition (D-09), all four cities, the null-RH sites (D-04/D-05), the MST-vs-CST
offset split, and the complete fail-loud suite (D-06/D-07, ROADMAP Success Criterion 4).

Purpose: Plan 09-01 proved the architecture end-to-end on one happy path. Everything that makes TCEQ
actually usable — and everything that makes it fail honestly instead of quietly — lives in the edges
this plan covers. ROADMAP Success Criterion 1 says "any of the four target cities," and Success
Criterion 4 is entirely about failure behaviour.

Output: a `fetch_tceq` that serves any window over any of the four sites, and raises clearly on every
condition that would otherwise produce a plausible-looking wrong answer.

**Still out of scope, deliberately:** no fallback to Synoptic or any other provider on a TCEQ failure
(D-06 — that is WX-06/Phase 10). This plan makes TCEQ fail loudly; making something catch that failure
is the next phase's job, and it requires no change to the raise behaviour built here.
</objective>

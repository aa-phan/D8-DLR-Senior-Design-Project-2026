## Design decisions (planner-locked)

| ID | Decision |
|----|----------|
| PD-1 | `make_study`'s loop wraps each city's whole step (prepare_city_base, screen, margin, dispatch, the details additions) in `try/except StudyFailed`, remembering `(city, stage, message)` and continuing with the next city. Before a city starts, `budget.elapsed() > budget.max_seconds` marks it not attempted. Only StudyFailed is collected: any other exception propagates at once (the finally still removes the temp directory). Index failures (`index_case`) are raised before the loop, unchanged |
| PD-2 | `combined_failure(outcomes, max_seconds)` takes the per-city outcomes in CITIES order (each `CITY_OK`, `("failed", stage, message)` or `("not_attempted",)`) and returns one StudyFailed. Message: `"%d of 4 cities failed" % n` (n counts failed and not attempted), then `"; "` and the clauses joined by `"; "`: `"<CITY>: ok"`, `"<CITY>: failed at <stage>: <text>"`, `"<CITY>: not attempted: run-time budget of %g s exceeded"`. `<text>` is the city's message with a leading `"<CITY>: "` or `"<CITY> "` dropped and a leading `"scaling: <CITY>: "` shortened to `"scaling: "`, then cut to `_CLAUSE_TEXT_CHARS = 180` characters. Stage: the first failed city's stage in CITIES order; `load_case` when no city failed but one was not attempted. `CITY_OK = "ok"` is a module constant |
| PD-3 | The run-wide contingency row cap of plan 04-03 applies only when all four cities succeeded. When all four succeed the result is unchanged from plan 04-03 |
| PD-4 | Worst case length: a 22-character header plus four clauses of at most 43 + 180 characters and the separators is under 950 characters, so `run_record`'s cut at 1000 after redaction never removes a city |

## Design decisions adopted from research (planner-locked)

| ID | Decision used here |
|----|--------------------|
| D-13 margin | Transfer stepping with bisection: evaluate(d) reloads the base, defines subsystem SINK_SID = 9 on the sink buses, scales their load by d MW at constant P/Q (scal_2, research A13), solves; ok means converged, not collapsed and the minimum monitored voltage at or above contingency_low_pu; stop at the first bad step or when the flow on the mapped branches reaches applied_mva; bisect to tolerance after a bad step. transfer_max_mw is a starting cap, not a stop: reaching it with no bound and the flow below the applied rating continues the walk for at most transfer_extra_steps more steps, and exhausting those fails the stage (no "max_transfer" outcome exists; plan-checker revision, replacing research's max_transfer lower bound, which could understate the limit). base_transfer_mw and limit_transfer_mw are the summed absolute from-end P of the mapped branches (assumption A18, new: the city's transfer is that sum); margin_mw = limit - base; margin_pct null below 0.1 MW of base; method "pv_curve" (the DDL's example label, used here for a load-transfer search, not a PSS/E PV-curve analysis); bounded_by in details; dlr_rating is a lower bound on the margin and says so. |
| D-13 limit | voltage_limit_mva = largest MAXMVA among the mapped branches at the last good point, per circuit, > 0, the same value in the margin and the dispatch row. |
| D-13 dispatch | dispatched_mva = min(applied_mva, voltage_limit_mva); amps via units.mva_to_circuit_amps; per-conductor amps = circuit amps / subconductors; dispatched_mw = dispatched_mva x 0.95 (dispatch.POWER_FACTOR_DEFAULT, a copy of config.POWER_FACTOR pinned by a test in psse_dispatch_test.py); limiting_factor dlr_rating when applied_mva <= voltage_limit_mva; a margin bounded by dlr_rating must give voltage_limit_mva >= applied_mva (ValueError otherwise, a tripwire); selection fields copied from the choice, the city_rating link only when the source is city_rating. |

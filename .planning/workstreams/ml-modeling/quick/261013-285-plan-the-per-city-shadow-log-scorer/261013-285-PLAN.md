S2. HEADLINE_FAMILY, fixed at 9 and not configurable (decision 2E). Each entry is an ordered pair (A, B) whose margin is mean|e_B| − mean|e_A|, so a positive margin means A has the lower MAE.
- Candidate vs candidate: (ml_wind_corrected, ml_cooling_corrected), (ml_wind_corrected, reference_ml_refit_24h), (ml_cooling_corrected, reference_ml_refit_24h).
- Correction vs raw: (ml_wind_corrected, raw_forecast), (ml_cooling_corrected, raw_forecast), (reference_ml_refit_24h, raw_forecast).
- Candidate vs persistence: (ml_wind_corrected, persistence_24h), (ml_cooling_corrected, persistence_24h), (reference_ml_refit_24h, persistence_24h).

The correction is Holm step-down at family alpha 0.05, with Bonferroni bound 0.05/9 at the first step. Per city, the same 9 pairs are reported with unadjusted 95% intervals, labelled "descriptive, uncorrected".

S3. Statistics (backend/forecast_shadow_score_stats.py).
- Pairing is complete case per pair. A row enters pair (A, B) only if both candidates have a scored value and truth exists (decision 10-i).
- Per-city descriptive results use the 1-D moving-block bootstrap over that city's paired rows ordered by valid_time. It must produce the same RNG draws as the frozen `forecast_experiment_audit.paired_block_bootstrap_margin`:
  - diff = |e_B| − |e_A|
  - rng = default_rng(seed)
  - starts = rng.integers(0, n − L + 1, size=(B, ceil(n/L)))
  - indices = starts + arange(L), truncated to n
  - the interval is np.percentile of the resample means at alpha/2 and 1 − alpha/2
  - a zero-ptp series gives a point mass
- The pooled headline uses the 6-hourly SLOT GRID: every slot from the earliest to the latest paired valid_time across cities, including empty slots. Two schemes:
  - (i) joint_time_block: draw moving blocks of L consecutive slots, ceil(S/L) of them, and include every paired (city, slot) row inside the drawn slots. The statistic is the sum of diffs divided by the count of included rows. This keeps same-time cross-city correlation.
  - (ii) city_cluster_two_stage: draw len(cities) cities with replacement, then take a moving-block slot resample within each drawn city at the same L. The statistic is the pooled row mean. It is documented as coarse with 4 clusters.
- Block length L is runnable when L <= S/2. The per-city equivalent is L <= n/2. Non-runnable lengths are skipped and listed.
- The two-sided bootstrap p is min(1, 2 · min((#means <= 0 + 1)/(B + 1), (#means >= 0 + 1)/(B + 1))).
- The headline p per test is the maximum p over both schemes and every runnable L (PD-04). The headline intervals are the widest 95% interval and the widest (1 − 0.05/9) interval over the same set.
- Holm: sort the 9 headline p ascending and reject while p(i) <= 0.05/(9 − i + 1), stopping at the first failure. Adjusted p(i) = max over j <= i of min(1, (9 − j + 1)·p(j)). A missing p is 1.0.
- Outcome per test:
  - insufficient_data if n_paired < MIN_PAIRED_ROWS, or if no L > 1 is runnable
  - favors_A / favors_B for the headline when Holm rejects (sign of the point margin)
  - favors_A / favors_B for a per-city result when the unadjusted 95% interval excludes 0 on the same side at every runnable L
  - otherwise inconclusive, with a reason (crosses_zero or block_length_sensitive)
- ESS:
  - Per city: the ACF-based effective n of the paired-difference series ordered by valid_time, n / (1 + 2·sum of rho_k), truncating at the first non-positive lag, capped at n/4 lags and clipped to [1, n]. It is documented as approximate for irregular log spacing.
  - Pooled: n_eff_boot = (sample variance of the pooled diffs) / (maximum bootstrap variance of the pooled mean across schemes). Also report the mean same-slot cross-city correlation rho_bar and the analytic design effect N / (1 + (m − 1)·rho_bar).
- MDD = (z(1 − alpha/2) + z(POWER)) · SE_boot, where SE_boot is the largest bootstrap SD over the reported set. rows_needed_for_5A = ceil(n · (MDD / 5)^2).
- Every family entry also reports:
  - the point margin
  - the relative MAE reduction 1 − MAE_A/MAE_B
  - per-city point margins
  - the number of cities whose point margin favours A, out of the cities with data
  - n_nominal
  - the flags from S7

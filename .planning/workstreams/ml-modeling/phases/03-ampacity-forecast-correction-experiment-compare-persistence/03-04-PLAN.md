<objective>
Build out the four arms that need no fitted model — 24-hour persistence, the NWP-wind-delta
baseline, the raw forecast through IEEE 738, and the oracle diagnostic — and promote scoring from
the tracer's single city to full corridor level via the weakest-link reduction Plan 03-02 already
extracted. All four score on the validation split at D-04's primary 24-hour lead, on a
byte-identical row index, for both project corridors.

Purpose: these four arms are the comparison's floor and ceiling. Arm 0 is the incumbent that Phase 1
found beats the shipped `"ml"` method, and at exactly 24 hours it is a *stronger* baseline than the
phase's original framing assumed — 24 hours is one full diurnal cycle, so T-24h shares the valid
time's solar angle and diurnal wind regime, making this the day-ago-same-hour baseline that
time-series literature treats as genuinely hard to beat (RESEARCH Pattern 4). Arm 6 is the ceiling:
it substitutes the *observed* future wind and therefore bounds the maximum benefit any wind
correction could ever capture. Landing both, plus the two cheap middle arms, before any estimator is
fitted means Plan 03-05's correction models are built against a comparison whose floor and ceiling
are already measured — and if the oracle's margin over the raw forecast turns out to be small, that
is decisive information about whether the ML correction arms are worth their complexity at all.

Output: a named arm registry, corridor-level frame assembly and weakest-link reduction, structural
oracle isolation, and a validation-split corridor report for both corridors.

**Why corridor level now (D-02).** Wave 1's tracer deliberately scored at city level and deferred
the weakest-link reduction rather than writing an inline elementwise minimum that a later plan would
delete — the duplicate-then-replace pattern the phase's research forbids. `corridor_weakest_link` has
existed since Plan 03-02, carries the non-obvious tie-break that an exact ampacity tie resolves to
the lower-index city, and is tested against a pre-refactor golden capture. This plan consumes it.

**Why the oracle's isolation is structural, not documentary (ROADMAP Success Criterion 2).** The
requirement is that the oracle is "labeled as non-forecast in every output surface." A label in
prose is a thing a future reader can miss and a future winner-selection loop will not read at all.
Putting it in a separate top-level report key instead of inside the candidate mapping means a
winner-selection step iterating the candidates cannot reach it, by construction.

**The test split stays locked.** Plan 03-03 installed a module-level lock defaulting to locked;
Plan 03-06 unlocks it in the same commit that lands the Expectation record. Arm 4's target
parameterization and arm 3's pooling choice are both explicitly scheduled to be decided on the
validation set, and this plan scores there too, so nothing in Wave 3 needs the test side.
</objective>

<objective>
Run the experiment for real, and write the record that says what it found — including, if that is the answer,
that nothing beat persistence.

Purpose: every plan before this one built machinery and proved it honest. This one produces the evidence and
commits to a reading of it. The phase exists because Phase 1's `"ml"` method lost to naive persistence and
`forecasting_model.md` explicitly declined to resolve what should replace it; this record is that resolution,
or an equally explicit statement that the seven approaches tested do not contain one. D-02 is unambiguous
that the second outcome is an acceptable and complete result: "explicitly stop and report if nothing beats
persistence rather than building probabilistic/regime/fusion layers on top of a losing result."

Output: a completed nine-section decision record, one appended cross-reference bullet in `forecasting_model.md`,
an ECE 464 disclosure entry, and a backlog note for the one piece of pre-existing debt this phase surfaced.

**The record must be readable by someone who was not here.** `forecasting_model.md` is the standard to match,
and it is a good one: a reader can reproduce its run from its Configuration table alone, can see both
candidates' numbers rather than only the winner's, and cannot mistake its narrow resolved question for the
broad one it left open. This record has a harder version of the same job, because it carries three passes at
two different leads over two different windows, plus a diagnostic that is not a forecast. The structure must
make confusing them difficult rather than merely discouraged: separate subsections, separate tables, and the
caveat that belongs to each pass printed with that pass rather than collected at the end.

**On the Expectation.** It was committed in Plan 03-05 before any machinery could measure against it. It is
not editable now. If the results suggest the bar was mis-specified — for instance if every arm clears clause
one and fails clause two, or vice versa — that is a genuine finding about the experiment's resolving power and
belongs in Open Questions. Adjusting the bar to fit the result would destroy the only reason for having stated
it in advance.
</objective>

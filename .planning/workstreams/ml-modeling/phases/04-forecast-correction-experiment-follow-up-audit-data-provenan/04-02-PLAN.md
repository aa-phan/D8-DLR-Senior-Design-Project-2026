<objective>
Audit the five provenance and leakage claims in D-01 against the code that makes them true or false,
and publish each with a citation and a disposition.

Purpose: Phase 3's frozen record makes four structural claims about its own integrity — the truth
source is a known archive, the lead time was measured rather than assumed, no arm reads its own
future, and the training labels postdate their features. Each of those is a claim about code. An
audit that reads the record and agrees has done nothing; the only useful output is a per-claim
verdict traceable to a file and a line, and where the claim is weaker than the record's framing, the
weaker version stated plainly.

Three of D-01's bullets are expected, from the research pass, to come back as non-issues or
narrower-than-suspected. That is a legitimate and valuable outcome and this plan must not manufacture
concern to avoid it — a "confirmed non-issue, and here is the code that makes it so" finding is worth
exactly as much to a future reader as a defect, because it stops the next audit from re-investigating
it. But two of D-01's bullets are expected to come back as real, currently-undisclosed limitations,
and this plan must not soften them: the calibration gate proves a narrower thing than
"available at decision time," and the leakage probe's demonstrated power was demonstrated somewhere
other than the code path that produced the frozen numbers.

Output: six executable audit routines, their tests, and a complete Data-provenance-re-audit section
in the audit record with one cited finding per D-01 bullet and a closing dispositions table.

**Why the leakage probe gets a two-mode re-run rather than a code reading.** The probe's own
docstring states that a callable which looks up a precomputed prediction by timestamp "would
trivially (and meaninglessly) report no change regardless of sabotage." The frozen test-set scoring
path builds its arm callables as prediction-column readers over an augmented frame. Whether that
matters is not a question to settle by reading — it is a question to settle by running the probe both
ways on real data and reporting the two outcomes side by side. If the prediction-column mode reports
every arm unchanged *including the oracle*, that is the measurement, and the oracle's silence is the
proof, because the oracle is the one arm that is designed to consume truth at the valid time.
</objective>

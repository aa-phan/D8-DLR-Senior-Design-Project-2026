<objective>
Correct the frozen record's language without touching the two sections that make it trustworthy, and
write the verdict this audit's own measurements support.

Purpose: Phase 3's record is honest about its process and overstated in its prose. It says
`ml_cooling_corrected` "wins on the held-out test set at both corridors"; the defensible claim is that
it had the lowest observed 24-hour mean absolute error in one test, on one held-out split, at one lead,
under one pinned weather model, chosen by largest point margin from six candidates scored once. It
reports margins clearing a 30-ampere practical threshold at Midland on the point estimate while the
interval's lower bound sits well below it — and the lower bound is the claim that matters if the number
is ever used to justify a production change. It reads the oracle's headroom as a ceiling on what is
achievable rather than as a bound on one intervention under an unrealizable input. And it measures
correction gains against persistence, which is a different comparison axis entirely: persistence
extrapolates the past, the correction arms correct a physics forecast, and the headroom attributable to
correction is the distance from the uncorrected raw forecast.

Two disciplines make this correction trustworthy rather than a rewrite. First, the Expectation and the
Measured results stay byte-identical, proven by extracting both sections from the committed file and
comparing them — those two sections are the entire no-peeking audit trail, and a record whose
Expectation can be edited after the answer is known has no audit trail at all. Second, the amendment is
append-only across the whole document: every line of the committed version must still appear, in order,
in the amended one. A reader must be able to see the original claim and its correction side by side and
judge for themselves.

One of D-04's six items is not a language question at all. It asks whether the low-wind behaviour
everyone keeps calling a sensitivity is in fact a code defect, and whether Phase 3's additive extraction
of the convective term weakened the physical floor. That is settled by measurement: sweep the ampacity
response to perpendicular wind across the crossover the previous plan measured, and check the response
is finite, monotone and continuous there; and re-verify the extraction's bit-identity directly against
the committed golden array from this phase's own code rather than citing Phase 3's own equality gate.

Output: a measured sensitivity-versus-instability finding, an independently re-verified bit-identity
claim, an appended amendment to the frozen record covering all six D-04 items under a byte-identity and
an append-only gate, and the audit's own corrected verdict and limitations.
</objective>

physics.

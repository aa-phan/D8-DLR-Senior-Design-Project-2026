<objective>
Answer the question Phase 3 left open, then write down exactly what an integration phase would need —
without building any of it, and without answering a product question this audit has no standing to
answer.

Purpose: the frozen record's Open Questions name a specific puzzle and defer it. The production
sixty-minute method's estimator family lost to naive persistence in Phase 1; the same family, refit at
twenty-four hours, beat persistence distinguishably at both corridors. The record offers a plausible
explanation — that the non-lag weather covariates carry real signal over a longer horizon even as the
lag features go stale — and says plainly that its own design does not isolate it and that an ablation
would be needed. That ablation is cheap on the data this phase already assembled, and D-05 asks for it
directly. Leaving it deferred a second time would be a choice, not a constraint.

The rest of D-05 is a specification, and specifications fail by being vague. "We need better data" is
not a data contract; a contract is a list of fields with names, types, units, cadences and owners, and
each field here has to be traceable to something this audit actually found. The decision-time
availability finding from Plan 04-02 is the clearest example: the reason the contract must carry
initialization, availability and valid timestamps as three distinct fields is that this audit
established that two retrospective archives cannot evidence when a value became available to a
consumer, and nothing short of an availability timestamp recorded at ingestion will fix that.

And one thing this phase must be careful not to do is answer a question it was not convened to answer.
Whether the product needs a sixty-minute forecast, a twenty-four-hour forecast, or both is a scope
question with real consequences, and the twenty-four-hour result does not settle it. It gets named,
assigned, and left open.

Output: a measured feature ablation with a testable cooling-rediscovery hypothesis; a field-level data
contract; a now-versus-later split with every recommendation in exactly one bucket; an outline of
packaging, runtime fetch, stale-data handling, fallback, shadow evaluation, monitoring and rollback; the
product question named as open; the ROADMAP's Phase 4 entry closed with a disposition per success
criterion; and the project's AI-use disclosure updated before the final commit.

**Why the ablation is a hypothesis test rather than a feature sweep.** D-05's first bullet asks
something sharper than "which features matter": it asks whether the refit model is implicitly
rediscovering a cooling-like correction or doing something else. That is a comparison between two
models' behaviour, not a ranking of feature subsets — so the ablation's covariate-only variant is
compared against the cooling correction's own predictions and residuals, and the answer is a
correlation with a number attached rather than a narrative.
</objective>

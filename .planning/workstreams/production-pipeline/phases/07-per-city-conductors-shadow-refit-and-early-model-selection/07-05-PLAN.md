<objective>
Write the signed v4 selection rule as a new, pure module: relative bars from mean truth, the three v3
evidence levels, 3-of-4 consistency, ABLATION/TEST vetoes, a stable tie order and an explicit no-pick
outcome (SEL-01's pre-registered rule, applied by SEL-04's reads).

Purpose: the v3 module compares a pooled margin with 5 A and falls back to raw_forecast or wind; v4
replaces that with 2% of the city's mean truth and "no pick" (D-06, D-07), keeps one method for all four
cities (D-04) and lets only FRESH decide while ABLATION and TEST veto (D-02). Keeping it a separate
module leaves the v3 module byte-identical. 07-07 wires it into both the headline port and the
live-system check.
Output: the module and its tests.
</objective>

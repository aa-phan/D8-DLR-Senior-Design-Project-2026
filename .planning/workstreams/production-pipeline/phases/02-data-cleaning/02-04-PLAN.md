<objective>
Finish CLEAN-02 on the consumer side: decide when a forecast built from history with gaps may gate
G6. The cleaning rules (02-03) already mark gaps and measure them; this plan adds
`data_cleaning.forecast_gap_reason(report, forecast_method)` and uses it in `city_pipeline` next to
the existing zero-width-band check, so a stale forecast (the newest usable row more than one slot
before the run) or an `ml` forecast whose positional lags span a gap is reported but not used by
G6. Then it closes the phase with a full gate.

Purpose: SC2 (missing hourly slots handled by the documented strategy before the forecast and
detector consume them; tests cover a skipped-cycle gap). OD-3 makes `ml` the live forecast method,
and its lag features assume consecutive hourly rows (`ampacity_forecast.build_ml_features` uses
positional `.shift(h)`), so a gap inside its lookback is the case that matters most in production.
Output: the guard and two constants in `backend/data_cleaning.py`, the wiring in
`backend/city_pipeline.py`, tests in `backend/tests/cleaning_pipeline_test.py`, and the policy
doc's "Forecast use across gaps" section.
</objective>

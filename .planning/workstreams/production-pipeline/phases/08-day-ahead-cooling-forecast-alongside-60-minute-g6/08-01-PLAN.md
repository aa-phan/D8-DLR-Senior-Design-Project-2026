<assumption_delta_decision>
Detector: pluralization ("alongside", "additional"): a second forecast output per city now exists
next to the 60-minute G6 forecast stored on each `city_rating` row.
- Primary noun for the new output: one day-ahead issue per (city, target hour), in its own table.
- Decision: `add-alongside` (owner-locked D-09: new table plus new view; `city_rating`,
  `latest_rating` and the 34-column contract untouched). The 60-minute forecast stays a field of the
  rating record because it gates G6; the day-ahead value never does.
- Accepted debt: the key (city, valid_time) holds one issue per target because `horizon_minutes` is
  CHECKed to 1440 and `forecast_method` to ml_cooling_corrected. Hourly issuance at several leads
  (deferred) or a second day-ahead method would force a promote to a key that includes the
  horizon or method, through a new migration and a contract revision.
- Invariant encoded now: the two DB CHECKs plus a contract test make any such plural change fail
  loudly instead of silently overwriting.
</assumption_delta_decision>

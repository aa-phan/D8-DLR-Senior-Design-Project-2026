## Member layout this plan writes (plan 01-09's reader relies on it)

`reference/<city>/presolve.raw`, `reference/<city>/solved.raw`, `reference/<city>/base.json`, `reference/<city>/outages.json`, `reference/<city>/outage_<nnn>.raw` (nnn is the zero-padded position in the outage list, never a bus number), `reference/<city>/margin.json`, `reference/<city>/margin_last_good.raw`, `reference/<city>/margin_first_bad.raw`; `<city>` is the CITIES name in lower case. JSON shapes (every file also carries the member marker key added by `add_json`):
- a solved state: `solve` (the SolveResult fields), `buses` (list of `number`, `type_code`, `base_kv`, `pu`, sorted by number), `line_flows` (list of `key`, `from_bus`, `to_bus`, `ckt`, `in_service`, `rate_mva`, `pct`, `p_mw`, `max_mva`, sorted by key), `branch_flows` (`status` ok or unavailable, `reason` (function and class when unavailable), `rows` of `key`, `is_transformer`, `in_service`, `p_mw`, `q_mvar`, `max_mva`), `machine_outputs` (`status`, `reason`, `rows` of `bus`, `id`, `in_service`, `p_mw`, `q_mvar`, `p_max_mw`, `p_min_mw`, `q_max_mvar`, `q_min_mvar`), `snapshot` (member name or null).
- `base.json`: `city`, `rate_set`, `thermal_basis`, `rating_source` ("static"), plus the solved state.
- `outages.json`: `city`, `status` (ok, none_selected or failed), `count`, `outages` (list of `position`, `key`, `from_bus`, `to_bus`, `ckt`, `converged`, `ierr`, `solved_code`, `meaning`, `call_failed` (function and class, or null), and the solved state fields or null).
- `margin.json`: `city`, `status`, `details` (the row's details dict), `bounded_by`, `last_good` and `first_bad` (each an increment plus a solved state, or null).

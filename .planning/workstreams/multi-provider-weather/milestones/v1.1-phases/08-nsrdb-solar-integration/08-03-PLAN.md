  <what-built>
`backend/nsrdb_provider.py` fetches GHI from NSRDB PSM v4 GOES CONUS for a lat/lon point, decomposing
the requested window into one request per calendar year, parsing the CSV's two-line metadata header,
range-checking against `PIPELINE_SCHEMA`, and raising a credential-redacted `ValueError` on any
rejected request. `weather_provider._fetch_grid` serves it as `provider="nsrdb"`, tagging rows with
`solar_source="nsrdb"` and a literal city key.

Every automated test in this phase mocks `requests.get`. That is correct for a test suite, and it
means no gate in this repository would notice a moved endpoint, a renamed parameter, or a GHI column
reported in a unit other than W/m². Two items were explicitly left open by research for this reason:
the exact `GHI Units` metadata spelling (A2) and the exact out-of-range-year rejection text (A3 /
Open Question 1).
  </what-built>

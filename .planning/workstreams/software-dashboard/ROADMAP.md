# Roadmap: Operator Dashboard

## Overview

Phases are not planned yet. The Software sub-team plans them with GSD against
[`REQUIREMENTS.md`](REQUIREMENTS.md) and [`docs/DASHBOARD-REQUIREMENTS.md`](docs/DASHBOARD-REQUIREMENTS.md).

Inputs the team has from day one:

- A live, read-only database with ratings for the four cities, and its contract
  ([`docs/integration/rest-api.md`](../../../docs/integration/rest-api.md)).
- Access instructions and three working read examples
  ([`docs/integration/software-team-handoff.md`](../../../docs/integration/software-team-handoff.md),
  [`docs/integration/examples/`](../../../docs/integration/examples/)).
- A plain-language column guide ([`docs/COLUMN-GUIDE.md`](docs/COLUMN-GUIDE.md)).
- Design tools and a suggested order of use (ch. 8 of the requirements document).

Decisions to make before or during the first phase are the open questions in ch. 11 of the
requirements document, in particular Q6 (framework, charting library, hosting).
